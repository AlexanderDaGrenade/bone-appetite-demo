Ask our team
Are Bone Appetite meals raw?

No — and that’s what makes them so gentle. Our meals are freshly cooked, not raw or processed like kibble. Every batch is prepared at low temperatures to keep nutrients and flavour while ensuring complete safety.

Why cooked food instead of kibble or raw?

Cooked food offers a middle path — fresh, digestible and safe. Dogs often have fewer stomach upsets, better hydration and a softer coat on gently cooked meals. We don’t claim miracles, but we do promise quality, transparency and love in every bite.

Do your recipes meet nutritional standards?

Yes. All our Complete recipes meet or exceed the FEDIAF 2024 Nutritional Guidelines for adult maintenance when fed as directed.

Do I need to cook it myself?

No cooking, no mess. The food arrives ready to serve — straight from the fridge, or slightly warmed with a spoon of warm water. Dogs adore the aroma when it’s a little warm.

Should I ask my vet before switching?

It’s always wise, especially if your dog is a puppy, senior, pregnant/lactating or has a medical condition. Bone Appetite meals are for healthy adult dogs and are not veterinary therapy.

What improvements might I see?
  • Healthier digestion
  • Smaller, less smelly stools
  • Shinier coat and steady energy

Every dog is unique, so results vary — but we’ll celebrate every small win with you.

Why not just cook at home?

You absolutely can — but balancing nutrients is tricky. Research shows that more than 90% of homemade dog-food recipes online miss essential nutrients or have unsafe ratios. We give you the goodness of home cooking without the worry.
